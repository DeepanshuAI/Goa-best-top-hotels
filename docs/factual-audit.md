# Factual audit — Goa, Considered

**Audit date:** 25 October 2026  
**Scope:** ten live hotel records, their visible profiles and comparison cells, date/rating/price claims, and the supplied property images.  
**Status key:** **Verified** = supported by the cited current primary page; **Partially verified** = the page supports the broad claim but not every detail/current operation; **Unverified** = no provenance or sufficient evidence was available; **Stale** = older wording no longer matches the current source.

The site does not publish prices, third-party ratings, review counts, awards, travel times, distances, first-hand visits or independent inspections. These claims were absent or removed. Facilities below mean “listed by the source checked,” not guaranteed available on a visitor’s dates. A live official property page is not proof of the property’s trading status; visitors should confirm directly.

## Images and attribution

Ten user-supplied image files from `public/` are used as small list thumbnails and on their matching hotel profiles. Their filenames and visible content appear to correspond to the properties. The originals contain no documented creator, capture date, source URL, release, license or attribution instructions in this project. **Status: Unverified for provenance, exact property identity and reuse rights.** Obtain and record rights/credit evidence before public launch; remove any image whose use cannot be confirmed. Original files are preserved. The largest source file, `Amrapali • House of Grace.webp`, is 2,082,836 bytes; generated WebP derivatives are used for the site, reducing that property’s displayed profile image to 220,170 bytes and its thumbnail to 41,490 bytes. Across all ten properties, the derivative set totals 1,417,952 bytes.

## Dates, reputation and editorial claims

- Every record’s `checked` date is `2026-10-25`, matching the source review recorded for this release. Regional labels are derived from matching record dates; individual hotel profiles show the relevant check date. The homepage no longer summarizes a check date; readers can find it on each profile.
- No publication/revision event is asserted beyond the date recorded in the project. The prototype has no published editorial review schedule or active visitor-report workflow. No prices, price-check dates, ratings, review counts, awards, ranking scores, independent visits or inspections appear in public hotel content.
- Property-type classifications and “best for”/regional trip-fit labels are editorial descriptions based on sourced characteristics, not formal hotel classifications or hotel claims. The site states this distinction.
- The correction/contact pages correctly say that this prototype has no monitored correction channel. No form submission is claimed or tested because no form exists.
