# Selection methodology and evidence

## Outcome

This release is an **editorial curated selection**, not a numeric ranking or objective “best” claim. The home page’s 1–10 numbers are an editorial display order only. They are not scores, star ratings, or an assertion that number one is measurably superior.

## What the display order means

The numbers identify a reading sequence for the ten profiles; the project did not apply a comparable formula or evidence standard that would justify ranking one property above another. “10 Best Hotels in Goa” is the search-intent framing for a mixed shortlist, not a claim that these are Goa’s ten objectively best hotels. The title, page introduction and selection note should keep that distinction visible wherever the list appears.

## Why no weighted score

The suggested categories (location fit, verified facilities, reputation, distinctiveness, value and transparency) cannot be assessed on a fair, comparable basis across every candidate from the available public evidence. Independent reputation evidence is not consistently attributable; current room prices depend on dates, room types, taxes and cancellation conditions; and the editors have not visited or audited the properties. Assigning numbers would create false precision. We therefore do not publish or internally invent numeric scores.

## Shortlisting rules

1. Discover candidates from government accommodation information, hospitality-group portfolios and credible travel publications.
2. Check current official property pages for a current name, location, property type and meaningful details.
3. Keep a candidate only when a distinct traveller use case and at least one useful, verified differentiator can be described.
4. Retain variety across North Goa, South Goa and a central base, plus boutique, family and resort-oriented stays.
5. State one practical trade-off and link readers to the primary source.
6. Use no prices, third-party ratings, review counts, awards, inspection claims or live availability absent a suitable source and dated methodology.

## Candidate pool

The initial 19-property discovery pool plus two GTDC residencies researched from official Goa Tourism pages (21 candidate leads total): Taj Fort Aguada Resort & Spa; Ahilya by the Sea; ITC Grand Goa Resort & Spa; Taj Exotica Resort & Spa; Taj Holiday Village Resort & Spa; Ronil Goa; The Park Calangute; The Westin Goa; W Goa; The St. Regis Goa Resort; Courtyard by Marriott Goa Colva; Alila Diwa Goa; Beleza by the Beach; Casa Jaali; The Postcard Velha; The Postcard Saligao; The Postcard Hideaway, Netravali; Cidade de Goa; Amrapali • House of Grace; Panaji Residency; and Calangute Residency.

The ten published properties are those with enough primary information reviewed in this work session to establish current official details and explain a distinct use case. The other eleven candidates were discovery leads, not fully evaluated exclusions. They are not represented as closed or inferior; they require additional checks before publication.

## Evidence ledger

The structured record `src/data/hotels.ts` stores the current official URL, source URL, check date, locality, facilities, editorial fit and trade-off for each selected property. “Best for,” rationale and trade-off are editorial interpretation. Other details are limited to claims found in the linked official source. The team should add one row per material fact if claims become more granular.

## Recheck cadence

Recheck official links, property names, region/locality, listed amenities and current operations before each annual content review, and promptly after a reported change. Reconfirm seasonal facilities close to the visitor’s travel dates. Update `checked` and the source log when records change.
