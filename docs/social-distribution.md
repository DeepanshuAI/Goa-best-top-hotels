## Search snippet hypothesis

Homepage title candidate: **Best Hotels in Goa: 10 Stays to Compare | Goa, Considered**. Description candidate: **Compare 10 curated Goa stays across North, South and Central Goa by location, listed facilities, trip fit and practical trade-offs.**

This wording presents the comparison benefit while retaining the curated-selection distinction. It is a proposal for observation, not a claim about a live Google result. Google can select title links and snippets from several page signals and may show different snippets for different queries. Once Search Console access is available, record baseline and later impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by query/page; change one element at a time and avoid attributing ordinary fluctuations to the wording without enough data.

## Distribution and link earning

Share the regional guide, the hotel-style comparison and the booking checklist where travellers already discuss Goa planning. Ask local publishers or itinerary writers for editorial corrections or references only when the specific resource is useful to their audience. Do not buy links, send bulk generic outreach or imply endorsements. Existing principles are in `docs/backlink-strategy.md`.
